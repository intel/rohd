WaveformTimeScale

WaveformTimeScale enum

The physical duration represented by one unit of Simulator.time.

This only controls the time metadata emitted to waveform files. It does not rescale timestamp numbers or change simulator scheduling. The supported values are the VCD-standard 1, 10, and 100 multiples from femtoseconds through seconds.
