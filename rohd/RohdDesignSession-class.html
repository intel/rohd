RohdDesignSession

RohdDesignSession class abstract interface

Transport-independent entry point for inspecting a live ROHD design.

Implementations maintain a canonical HierarchyService and return its occurrence objects as handles. DTD, MCP, and other transports serialize occurrence addresses or paths at their boundary, then resolve them back to handles owned by the receiving session. All handles are valid only while status reports the same RohdDesignStatus.epoch.
